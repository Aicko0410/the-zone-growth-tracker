Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

About this application

The Zone Growth Tracker is a personal dashboard for tracking TikTok account growth and video performance.

Information collected

With user authorization, the application may access TikTok profile information, follower counts, and public video statistics through TikTok's API.

How information is used

Information is used solely to display account statistics and track growth.

Data storage

Manually entered statistics are stored locally in the user's browser. Authorized TikTok data may be processed to display statistics in the dashboard.

Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

Data deletion

Users can clear locally stored data through their browser settings and revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the official The Zone TikTok account.
